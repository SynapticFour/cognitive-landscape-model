# What this repository is not

- Not a diagnostic instrument, not a medical device, and not medical advice. The ethics notes are in [ethics.md](ethics.md). Outputs must not be used for diagnosis, treatment, employment screening, or high-stakes educational placement.
- Not the public questionnaire. That instrument is [PCMS](https://github.com/SynapticFour/perceptual-cognitive-mapping-system), published at [https://map.synapticfour.com](https://map.synapticfour.com).
- Not the research programme. Principles and the validation reports live in the [Cognitive Landscape Research Programme](https://github.com/SynapticFour/cognitive-landscape-research-programme).
- Not a store of participant data. This library does not collect answers. An integrator who saves states has to provide consent and retention. See [ethics.md](ethics.md).
- Not external psychometric validity. Package tests show that the code agrees with itself. See [STATUS.md](STATUS.md).

Latent priors and influence weights in the presets are design- and literature-informed. The draft framework report says they are not parameters from a dedicated normative corpus.
